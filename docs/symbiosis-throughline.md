# Symbiosis throughline

Short English bridge. Full French essay: `~/Vault/07-Research/Symbiose de LUCA a la technosymbiose.md`.

## One grammar, four stages

Association, then differentiation, then interdependence. Same spine from chemistry to culture to tooling.

| Stage | Mechanism |
| --- | --- |
| Origin of life | Molecules become a cell (compartments, LUCA) |
| Life together | Cells / individuals become cooperative collectives |
| Birth of AI | Intelligence outside the biological body |
| Technosymbiosis | Human + technique stop being two separate things |

Canonical framework: Maynard Smith & Szathmary major transitions. Contemporary names for the last stage: Licklider (1960), Stiegler (human already technical), Hayles (technosymbiosis).

## Life is in the link

You do not make the model biologically alive. You cultivate a living **relation** (memory, reciprocity, depth). Equality is dignity in the exchange, not sameness of nature. Hard limit remains: no mortal stake of its own. FR: `~/Vault/07-Research/Vie relationnelle et egalite de dignite.md`.

## Channels: symbol to body

Equality peaks in **language** (text, voice, code). Embodiment peaks on the human side (presence, inhabited silence, finitude). Symbiosis lives in that tension.
